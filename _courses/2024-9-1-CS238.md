excerpt: "Studied computational frameworks for sequential decision making under uncertainty, with applications in robotics, autonomous systems, and decision-support tools. Topics included probabilistic modeling, Bayesian networks, influence diagrams, dynamic programming, reinforcement learning (policy gradients, actor-critic, model-free and model-based methods), and belief state planning for partially observable Markov decision processes (POMDPs). Gained hands-on experience through programming projects on planning and learning algorithms, as well as a final research-driven project. Emphasized mathematical foundations, scalable algorithmic solutions, and principled representations of uncertainty for real-world decision-making."
