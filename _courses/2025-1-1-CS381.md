excerpt: "Studied state-of-the-art approaches in sensorimotor learning for embodied AI, focusing on how autonomous agents perceive their environment, learn policies, and adapt through physical interaction. Covered topics such as dense visual representations, self-supervised vision transformers, policy learning with diffusion models, vision-language-action frameworks, benchmarking from human demonstrations, compliance control, and differentiable hardware design. The course emphasized critical analysis of recent research papers through structured in-class discussions, highlighting the challenges and opportunities unique to embodied intelligence."
